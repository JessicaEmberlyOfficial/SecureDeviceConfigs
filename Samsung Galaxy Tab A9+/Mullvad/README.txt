Please follow these steps accordingly:
1. Open Mullvad.
2. Go to settings.
3. Enable DAITA.
4. Enable Multihop.
5. Go to VPN Settings.
6. Go to DNS settings, and turn on all applicable options.
7. Go back to VPN Settings.
8. Go to Auto-connect & Lockdown mode, and enable both options.
